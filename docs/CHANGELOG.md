# 0.2.0 — A world worth tasting

- Expanded from 12 ingredients and 9 recipes to **30 ingredients and 32 recipes**.
- Added seafood and patisserie suppliers, six-tier ingredient chains, and two-ingredient cooking.
- Added Rome, Mexico City, Istanbul, and Seoul, bringing the journey to **eight destinations**.
- Created **52 new SVG artworks**: 18 ingredients, 23 dishes, two suppliers, eight postcards, and a chef medal.
- Added **20 chef levels**, supplier/recipe unlocks, claimable level rewards, and **ten goals**.
- Added an interactive, resumable **six-step tutorial**, replay/skip options, and a one-time welcome gift.
- Added a coin market, pantry expansion to 20 slots, and item selling.
- Added recipe filters, ingredient discovery tracking, item previews, merge hints, and cooking-ready badges.
- Improved selection behavior, preserved scroll positions, touch tooltips, notification placement, fades, and gentle-motion settings.
- Added save-schema migration and regression tests for existing player progress.
